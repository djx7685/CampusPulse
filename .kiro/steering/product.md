# CampusPulse

## Product goal
CampusPulse helps students discover campus events, find student clubs and study groups, and exchange useful items through one approachable community hub.

## Intended audience
Undergraduate and graduate students who want to participate in campus life without checking several disconnected channels.

## Experience principles
- Make the next useful action easy to spot.
- Keep information scannable and friendly on desktop and mobile.
- Let playful campus energy support clarity rather than obscure it.
- Be transparent that demo interactions are local and do not create real posts or accounts.

## Demo scope
The current prototype includes a campus overview, event cards, clubs, a study circle, marketplace listings, save interactions, and a create menu. It uses sample data and does not connect to university systems or store student information.
